# Better Yet: Higgsfield image prompt pack (v2 brand direction)

Brand direction proposed by Christianna, 26 Oct 2026, for Rob's review. Boards are in
`references/` (visual identity board and visual references board).

**The look in one line:** black-and-white candid photography of real people at work,
electric blue `#0047FF` used as a wash, a panel or a hand-drawn mark, off-white `#F7F6F3`
backgrounds. No robots, no glowing circuitry, no stock-photo smiles.

## Status (26 Oct 2026)

- Workspace: starter plan, 60.5 credits.
- Nothing from this pack has been generated yet. History holds Nano Banana Flash (15 to 16 Oct),
  GPT Image 2.5 (18 Oct) and Nano Banana 2 (21 Oct) runs, all from the v1 direction.
  No Soul 2.0 runs exist.
- Budget: roughly 10 to 12 keepers on the current credits. Higgsfield does not publish a
  per-image cost through the API, so check the credit counter after the first slot. Generate
  the hero strip and the four Who we help cards first (7 images), then About and
  "A Friday, after", then the blue panels only if credits remain.

## How to use

1. In Higgsfield pick **Soul 2.0** for people photos (realistic, 2k) or **GPT Image 2**
   when text or a flat blue panel must render cleanly.
2. Set the aspect ratio listed on the slot. Soul 2.0 does not offer 4:5; use 3:4 for the
   "A Friday, after" card and crop to 4:5 on the site.
3. Paste the full prompt (scene plus suffix). Generate 2 to 4 variants. Keep the one that
   looks least posed.
4. Download the keeper and name it with the slot code (for example `hero-01-ute.jpg`).

**Style suffix.** Already appended to every prompt below. If you rewrite a prompt, keep it:

```
black and white documentary photograph, natural window light, 35mm, candid, slight film grain, shallow depth of field, Australian small business, no text, no logos, no watermarks
```

## 6. Blue-wash variants

Generate the black-and-white image first, then edit it in Higgsfield (image reference plus
this prompt):

```
apply a flat electric blue #0047FF duotone over the whole photo, high contrast, blue shadows and off-white highlights
```

Keep both versions. The site uses the black-and-white one and shows the blue wash on hover.

## What to avoid

Robots, holograms, glowing brains, circuit boards, people pointing at screens in suits,
perfect teeth, text on screens we can't control, anyone looking into the lens.
